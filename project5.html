Visual Design
for Maslinica Hotels & Resort

Project Overview

This project focused on creating a variety of promotional visuals for Maslinica Hotels & Resort. The materials included designs for hotel TVs, as well as A4 and A5 flyers and leaflets that were often handed out at reception. Additionally, many of these visuals were adapted for use across social media, the official website, and newsletters. The goal was to ensure a cohesive and visually engaging presentation across all communication channels.

Work Process

  • Created multiple promotional designs tailored for print, digital screens, and online platforms.
  • Used Canva, Photoshop, and Illustrator to produce cohesive and brand-aligned visuals.
  • Prepared A4 and A5 flyers and leaflets for on-site guest distribution.
  • Adapted visuals for use on social media, hotel website, and newsletters.
  • Ensured all materials maintained a consistent visual identity across mediums.

Result

The final collection of visuals successfully enhanced the resort’s brand visibility both online and on-site. Guests were more informed about hotel services through printed materials and digital displays, while the consistent branding across all platforms strengthened Maslinica Hotels & Resort’s visual identity.
