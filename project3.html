Web & Newsletter Design
for Maslinica Hotels & Resort

Project Overview

I developed a complete offer proposal for Maslinica Hotels & Resort, focusing on web design and newsletter visuals. The project aimed to modernize the resort’s digital communication and bring consistency between its web and email presence. Visual assets were created in Canva, while the web and newsletter layouts were designed in Figma before being implemented on the official website using Lauris CMS.

Tools Used

Canva, Figma, Lauris CMS

Web design layout created in Figma

Newsletter visuals designed in Canva

Work Process

  • Started by researching the brand’s identity and defining a consistent visual direction.
  • Created promotional visuals and mockups in Canva to present the proposal concept.
  • Designed the web and newsletter layouts in Figma, focusing on clarity, usability, and alignment with the resort’s brand aesthetics.
  • Implemented the approved design within Lauris CMS, ensuring responsive and consistent presentation across platforms.

Result

The final outcome successfully unified the visual identity of Maslinica Hotels & Resort’s online presence. The redesigned website and newsletter visuals improved communication coherence, enhanced brand perception, and provided a clean, engaging design tailored to the hospitality experience.
