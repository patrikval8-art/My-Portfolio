3D Map for Maslinica Hotels & Resort

Project Overview

I created a detailed 3D model of a resort in Blender. The project was particularly challenging due to the resort’s large scale and complexity. The model was developed in two versions to explore different approaches and highlight various aspects of the resort.

Tools Used

Blender, Photoshop

Version 1

Version 2

Resort legend

Work Process

  • Version 1 focused on visual detail, with extensive time spent in Photoshop to enhance textures and presentation.
  • Version 2 prioritized practicality and included more points of interest for better usability.
  • Both versions were refined and reviewed to balance aesthetics and functionality in the final outcome.

Result

The final outcome consists of two complementary models: Version 1 emphasizes polished visuals, while Version 2 provides a more practical and comprehensive representation of the resort. Together, they showcase both aesthetic quality and functional design considerations.
