Boat Tour Promo Design
for Maslinica Hotels & Resort

Project Overview

For this project, I created a promotional campaign for Maslinica Hotels & Resort highlighting their boat tour experience. The campaign consisted of six promotional images designed in Canva, each focusing on different aspects of the tour — from scenic views to onboard experiences. These visuals were then animated in Canva to create a short promotional video displayed on the hotel’s in-room and lobby TVs. The videos were managed online to ensure consistent updates and scheduling across all hotel displays. This was one of several promotional video projects produced for the resort.

Work Process

  • Designed six promotional visuals in Canva, aligning them with the hotel’s branding and color palette.
  • Animated the visuals to create an engaging short video advertisement.
  • Formatted the video for optimal display on hotel TV screens and digital signage.
  • Uploaded and managed the videos online to ensure they were displayed across all hotel TVs simultaneously.
  • Maintained consistent style and tone across multiple promotional video campaigns.

Result

The campaign successfully promoted the hotel’s boat tour experience through visually appealing and dynamic content. The videos attracted guest attention in common areas and rooms, enhancing awareness of hotel activities and contributing to increased participation in the tours. This project demonstrated the effectiveness of cohesive digital promotion across multiple screens.
