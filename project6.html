Menu Design
for Maslinica Hotels & Resort

Project Overview

This project involved creating menu designs for various restaurants and bars within Maslinica Hotels & Resort. Each menu was uniquely styled to complement the atmosphere and theme of the specific venue, ensuring a cohesive yet distinctive visual identity across the resort’s dining experiences. The focus was on readability, elegance, and brand consistency throughout the entire collection.

Tools Used

Canva, Adobe Illustrator, Adobe Photoshop

Main restaurant menu designed in Illustrator

Bar menu created to fit a relaxed atmosphere

Pool bar menu designed in Canva for digital and print use

Work Process

  • Reviewed the branding and interior design of each venue to define the appropriate visual style.
  • Created layouts in Canva, Illustrator, and Photoshop, focusing on clarity and balance.
  • Coordinated with management for feedback and final content updates.
  • Optimized final files for both high-quality print and digital screen display.

Result

The resulting menus enhanced the guest experience by combining aesthetic appeal with easy navigation. Each menu complemented its environment and contributed to a consistent, high-end brand image across all Maslinica Hotels & Resort dining locations.
